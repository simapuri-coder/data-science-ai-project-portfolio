# Medicon Dose Testing

## Problem statement

Medicon needs to evaluate the quality of a new vaccine batch. The analysis estimates the probability of unsatisfactory doses, studies the distribution of time-to-effect, and calculates a confidence interval for the population mean.

## Analytical objectives

- Model satisfactory and unsatisfactory outcomes with the binomial distribution.
- Calculate exact, cumulative, and tail probabilities for dose quality.
- Analyze dose time-to-effect with the normal distribution.
- Estimate the population mean using a 95% confidence interval.

## Techniques

Python, NumPy, pandas, SciPy, probability distributions, percentiles, and confidence intervals.
