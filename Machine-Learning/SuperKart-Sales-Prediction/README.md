# SuperKart Sales Prediction

## Problem statement

SuperKart wants to forecast product-level sales revenue across its supermarkets and food marts. The prediction will support outlet strategy, city-tier planning, and inventory decisions for the next quarter.

## Analytical objectives

- Explore product, store, location, and sales characteristics.
- Engineer and preprocess numeric and categorical predictors.
- Train and compare regression models.
- Tune the strongest candidates and interpret their business value.

## Techniques

EDA, preprocessing pipelines, one-hot encoding, regression trees, ensemble models, cross-validation, hyperparameter tuning, and regression metrics.
